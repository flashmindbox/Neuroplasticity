# Roadmap to CSE 2027

[← Handbook](README.md)

Dates assume the UPSC 2027 calendar (Prelims 23 May 2027, Mains from 20 Aug 2027). If UPSC changes dates,
change them in the app's Settings — the phases move automatically.

| Phase | Dates | Programme dose | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Diagnose & install** | First 2 weeks | 30 min/day | Baseline tests, doctor visit, Personal Charter, keystone habits |
| **1. Build** | Weeks 3–12 (≈ Nov–Jan) | 45 → 75 min/day | Self-regulation, thinking drills, vision & identity, first experiments |
| **2. Prelims mind** | ≈ Feb → 22 May | 60 min/day; taper last 10 days | Calibration & risk discipline, mock composure, comparison hygiene |
| **3. Mains mind** | 24 May → 19 Aug | 60–75 min/day; taper last 10 days | Stamina, structured expression at speed, essay creativity, ethical reasoning |
| **4. Personality Test mind** | After Mains → interview | 45–90 min/day | Communication, presence, balanced opinions, leadership stories, mock boards |

**Deload weeks:** roughly every sixth week, halve the programme dose (not your study). Analogy from sports
periodisation — label it as such and check your data.

## Phase 0 — Diagnose & install (2 weeks)
- Fill the **Personal Charter**: purpose statement, Plan B, support contacts, your 2–3 active targets.
- **Doctor visit + blood tests** (or results from the last ~3 months) — see [Brain clarity](modules/L0-brain-clarity.md).
- **Baseline tests** — [Assessments](assessments/baseline-and-monthly.md): Brain Check, focus test, recorded talk, thinking drill, PHQ-9/GAD-7, 13-dimension self-rating.
- **Fog audit** — your first personal experiment (14 days of clarity ratings).
- **Keystone habits** — fixed wake time, morning light + movement, phone out of the study room, nightly 5-minute journal.

## Phase 1 — Build (≈ 10 weeks)
- Attention training 10 → 20 min/day; focus blocks 25 → 50 → 90 min.
- One thinking drill per day (rotating types).
- Vision work: process simulation daily; WOOP for each 2-week sprint.
- Recruit **one accountability partner** by week 4 and identify a **counsellor option** before you need one.
- First two personal experiments (e.g., exercise timing, caffeine cut-off).
- Expect the first **motivation dip** around January ("messy middle") — it is in the plan.

## Phase 2 — Prelims mind (≈ 15 weeks)
- Confidence tagging on every mock question; calibration review after each mock.
- Mock-day routine rehearsed at exam time (9:30 and 14:30).
- Comparison hygiene: no score-sharing groups the night before or after a mock.
- Dose taper in the final 10 days: keep sleep, exercise, short attention practice; drop everything new.
- **Exam-day if-then plans** written by 1 May.

## Phase 3 — Mains mind (≈ 12 weeks)
- Prelims result-wait stress protocol (results are typically out within weeks; plan for uncertainty).
- 3-hour simulations, building to the **5-day stamina rehearsal** (two papers a day).
- Thinking drills speed up: 7 lenses in 60 s; 1-minute structures.
- Ethical dilemmas twice a week; essay-brainstorm drill weekly.

## Phase 4 — Personality Test mind
- Weekly AI mock boards from your profile; recorded talks 3×/week.
- Leadership-story bank finalised (real stories collected since Phase 1).
- Balanced-opinion drills on current issues; graceful "I don't know".
- Waiting-period protocol: keep structure, sleep and exercise during the long gaps between results.
