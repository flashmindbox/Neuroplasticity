# Daily protocol

[← Handbook](README.md)

The full protocol takes **60–90 minutes** of dedicated time, plus habits embedded in your study day. In Phase 0
it is about 30 minutes. The app shows today's version, adjusted for your phase, week and active targets.

## Morning (20–30 min)
1. **Fixed wake time** — same time every day, aligned to the exam slots (aim to be fully alert by 9:30).
2. **Light + movement (5–10 min)** — outdoor daylight soon after waking; a short walk or mobility routine.
3. **Attention training (10 → 20 min)** — breath-focused attention practice ([Focus & discipline](modules/L1-focus-discipline.md)).
4. **Vision & process simulation (2 min)** — read your purpose statement; then mentally rehearse *how* today's
   hardest task will go, including the moment you'll want to quit and what you'll do then.
5. **Today's if-then plan (1 min)** — one implementation intention for today's most likely obstacle.

## During study (embedded, ~0 extra minutes)
- **Focus-block protocol** for every study block: write the intention → phone out of the room → tally each
  noticed mind-wander → one-line review at the end.
- **Breathing reset (1–2 min)** between blocks: a few slow cyclic sighs (long exhale).
- **Real breaks**: stand up, move, look into the distance; no phone feeds.

## Midday (15 min)
- **One thinking drill** — rotating: 7-lens, steelman, causal chain, think-like-a-DM, 1-minute structure,
  calibration quiz ([drill banks](drills/thinking-prompts.md)). Score yourself first; then (optionally) ask the AI.

## Evening
- **Exercise 30–45 min** — brisk walk, run, cycling, sport. Not optional; it is part of the training.
- **Recorded talk (3×/week, 5 min)** — 2 minutes on a [talk topic](drills/talk-topics.md), then review.
- **Reflection journal (10 min)** — one win · one emotion named · one lesson · tomorrow's if-then · (optional) gratitude.
- **Quick log (≤ 3 min)** — sleep, exercise, stress, mood, energy, clarity, caffeine cut-off, screen time.
- **Wind-down** — screens off 45–60 minutes before your fixed bedtime.

## Minimum viable day (≈ 5 min)
For illness, travel, bad news or exhaustion. It keeps the chain unbroken:
1. 1 minute of breathing.
2. One line in the journal.
3. One tiny version of your top target (e.g., a single 10-minute focus block).

Never miss two days in a row. A minimum viable day counts.

## Weekly additions
- One **stretch challenge** (slightly outside your comfort zone).
- One **service or leadership action**.
- One **phone-free long walk**.
- The **Sunday review** ([rituals](03-weekly-monthly-rituals.md)).
