# Weekly & monthly rituals

[← Handbook](README.md)

## Sunday review (20–30 min)

The app pre-fills your numbers. Answer honestly:

1. **Numbers:** habit completion %, focus minutes, average sleep, exercise days, average stress, clarity, mock calibration (if any).
2. **What worked?** One thing to keep.
3. **What didn't?** One thing to change — and the specific change.
4. **Rule check:** did any pre-committed decision rule fire? (see [Failure modes](06-failure-modes.md)) Act on it.
5. **Motivation check:** which source is lowest — purpose, progress, connection or energy? ([Motivation engine](modules/L2-motivation.md))
6. **Next week:** stretch challenge, service action, and the one if-then plan that matters most.
7. **Accountability:** share the summary with your partner (once you have one).

**Rule:** the review adjusts the *plan*, never your *worth*. Bad weeks are data.

## 2-week sprints

Each sprint has a finish line (e.g., "10 focus blocks of 50 min", "6 recorded talks"). Goals feel more motivating
as you approach a finish line (Kivetz et al., 2006), so short sprints give you many finish lines across ten months.
Start each sprint with a WOOP ([Vision & identity](modules/L2-vision-identity.md)); use the first Monday as a
fresh start (Dai et al., 2014).

## Monthly (first Sunday, ~60 min)

1. **Re-test:** Brain Check, focus test, recorded talk (external rating), thinking drill (AI rating), PHQ-9 and GAD-7.
2. **Rate the 13 dimensions** using the [level descriptors](00-foundations/target-profile.md). Compare with external ratings.
3. **Experiments:** close finished experiments; decide keep/drop; start the next one.
4. **Targets:** promote an automatic target to "maintenance" and choose a new active target (max 2–3).
5. **Programme retrospective:** what in the programme itself should change? Write it in the [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
6. **Backup:** export your data from the app's Settings.
