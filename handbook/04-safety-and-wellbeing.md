# Safety & wellbeing

[← Handbook](README.md)

UPSC preparation combines high stakes, long isolation, uncertainty and comparison. Distress during it is common
and is not a weakness. This programme is **not therapy** and **not medical care**. This page tells you when to
get help that the programme cannot give.

## If you are in crisis now

If you are thinking about harming yourself or feel unsafe:
- **Tele-MANAS (Government of India, free, 24×7): 14416** or **1-800-891-4416**
- **Emergency: 112**
- Tell one trusted person *now*. You do not have to explain everything.

## Monthly check-ins (in the app)

The app includes two widely used, free-to-use questionnaires:
- **PHQ-9** for depressive symptoms (Kroenke et al., 2001)
- **GAD-7** for anxiety symptoms (Spitzer et al., 2006)

They are **screening tools, not diagnoses**. The app interprets them with fixed rules (no AI involved):

| Result | What the app does | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| PHQ-9 or GAD-7 score 0–9 | Records it | Continue; watch the trend |
| Score 10–14 | Shows "talk to a professional" | Book a counsellor or doctor within 2 weeks; tell your accountability partner |
| Score ≥ 15 | Shows "talk to a professional soon" | Book within days; consider lowering programme dose |
| PHQ-9 item 9 (thoughts of being better off dead or hurting yourself) **above 0** | Shows the crisis contacts immediately, whatever the total | Contact Tele-MANAS or a professional **today** |

## Warning signs to act on — even without a questionnaire

- Sleep badly disrupted for more than two weeks.
- Loss of interest in things you normally enjoy; persistent hopelessness.
- Withdrawing from everyone.
- Using alcohol or other substances to cope.
- Thoughts that life is not worth living.

## Prepare before you need it (Phase 0–1 tasks)

1. Save Tele-MANAS (14416) in your phone.
2. Identify one **counsellor option**: a college counselling centre, a hospital psychology department, or Tele-MANAS referral.
3. Name one **trusted person** who will hear the truth from you.
4. Agree with family on expectations and quiet hours (social support buffers stress — Cohen & Wills, 1985).

## Protecting your identity

Your worth is not your rank. Write your **Plan B** in the Charter — what a good, meaningful life looks like if
CSE does not work out this year. This is not pessimism; it lowers the threat level, and lower threat improves
performance ([Confidence & resilience](modules/L2-confidence-resilience.md)).
