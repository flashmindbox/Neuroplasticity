# Rewire — The Topper Mind Programme

A 10-month, evidence-based programme to rewire **how you think, focus, handle pressure, lead and perform** —
built for one aspirant (you) targeting a **top-10 rank in UPSC CSE 2027** (optional: PSIR).

> This programme does **not** teach syllabus content. Your study programme does that.
> Rewire trains the brain and the person that *use* the content: the attention that holds for a 3-hour paper,
> the judgement that decides when to guess in Prelims, the calm that writes clearly at question 17 of 20,
> and the presence a Personality Test board looks for.

## What "rewiring" honestly means

Your brain changes with what you **repeatedly do** — not with what you read, wish or visualise alone.
Every part of this programme is therefore a *behaviour you practise*, a *dose* (how much, how often), and a
*marker* that tells you whether it is working. Read [How the brain changes](00-foundations/how-the-brain-changes.md) first.

No programme can guarantee a rank. What this one is designed to guarantee is a **self-correcting system**:
it uses the strongest available evidence, measures you objectively, notices early when something is not
working, and survives bad days, bad months and even a bad result. See [Failure modes](06-failure-modes.md).

## How to use this handbook

1. **Week 1:** read the Foundations, fill your **Personal Charter** in the app, book a doctor visit
   ([Brain clarity](modules/L0-brain-clarity.md)), and take the baseline tests ([Assessments](assessments/baseline-and-monthly.md)).
2. **Every day:** follow the [Daily protocol](02-daily-protocol.md) — the app's Today screen walks you through
   it one step at a time, sized to how you are that morning.
3. **Every week:** the [Sunday review](03-weekly-monthly-rituals.md). **Every month:** re-test, rate the 13 dimensions, review the programme itself.
4. **Always:** keep only **2–3 active targets** at a time. More is not better — it backfires ([why](00-foundations/programme-rules.md)).

## An honest limit

This handbook was assembled by an AI from published research. It is not a substitute for a doctor, a
counsellor or an experienced mentor. Before you rely on it heavily, ask a counselling psychologist and someone
who has reached the UPSC interview stage to read it and challenge it. Record what they change in the [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md).
