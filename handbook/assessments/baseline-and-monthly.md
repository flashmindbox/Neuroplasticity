# Assessments — baseline & monthly

[← Handbook](../README.md)

Take every test once in **Phase 0** (baseline) and then on the **first Sunday of each month**. Same time of day,
similar conditions (rested, not right after a mock). All tests are in the app under **Tests**.

## Objective tests

| Test | What it measures | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Brain Check** (weekly) | Reaction time, processing speed (symbol match), digit span | ~3 min | First 4 sessions = practice period ([Brain clarity](../modules/L0-brain-clarity.md)) |
| **Focus test** | Sustained attention: read a demanding text for up to 20 min; the app probes "were you on task?" at random moments | 10–20 min | Score = % of probes on task |
| **Multi-angle fluency** | Angles generated in 90 s on a new prompt | 2 min | Rated by rubric + AI |
| **Recorded talk** | Words/min, fillers/min, rubric | 3 min | Self + AI rating |
| **Writing speed** | Hand-written words/min at legible quality | 10 min | Log the number |
| **Calibration** | From your last mocks (gap, Brier) | — | Automatic |
| **Stamina** (from Phase 3) | Quality in final 30 min vs first 30 min of a 3-hour simulation | 3 h | Self + rubric |

## Wellbeing screening (monthly)

- **PHQ-9** (Kroenke et al., 2001) and **GAD-7** (Spitzer et al., 2006) — free to use; interpreted with fixed rules
  ([Safety & wellbeing](../04-safety-and-wellbeing.md)). Screening tools, not diagnoses.

## Self-ratings (monthly)

- **13-dimension rating** using the [level descriptors](../00-foundations/target-profile.md) — 1–10 each.
- Where an external rating exists (AI or a person), the app shows the **gap**.

Validated questionnaires for stress, self-efficacy, self-control and grit exist (e.g., the Perceived Stress Scale)
and you may take them separately; the app does not reproduce them, and relies on its own items and objective tests.

## Reading your results

- Look at **4-week trends**, not single points.
- Expect practice effects in the first weeks of any test.
- A sudden drop has a reason more often than not — sleep, illness, a bad week. Look for it before concluding anything.
- Unexplained Brain Check decline for 2+ weeks → doctor ([Failure modes](../06-failure-modes.md)).
