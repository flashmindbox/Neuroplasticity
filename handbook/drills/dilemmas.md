# Ethical dilemma bank

[← Handbook](../README.md)

For the weekly dilemma ([Leadership & integrity](../modules/L4-leadership-integrity.md)). For each:
stakeholders · options (at least three) · principles at stake · your decision · how you would explain it to the
people affected · what you would do to prevent it happening again. The app reads every bullet below.

## Dilemmas

- As a District Collector, you find that a contractor delivering a vital flood-relief project on time is also bribing junior officials. Stopping him now delays relief before the monsoon.
- Your close friend, now a junior officer under you, asks you to overlook a small irregularity in his file "just this once" because his family is in crisis.
- A local leader offers to mobilise villagers for your district's vaccination drive, but only if you attend his party's event.
- During an inspection you discover that a well-run NGO partner has inflated beneficiary numbers to secure funding it genuinely spends on the poor.
- Your superior asks you to fast-track approval of a project that you believe violates environmental norms; refusing may cost you your posting.
- A journalist asks you, off the record, about a sensitive investigation that the public arguably has a right to know about.
- A family begs you to waive the eligibility criteria for a scheme because they narrowly miss the cut-off and are clearly destitute.
- You learn that a colleague is suffering from depression and is making serious errors in land records. He asks you not to report it.
- To meet an ambitious target for toilet construction, field staff are reporting incomplete toilets as complete. Your performance review depends on this target.
- A tribal community refuses relocation from a tiger reserve core area; the law supports relocation, and they say their livelihood and identity depend on staying.
- A demolition drive against illegal encroachments will make several poor families homeless during the school year.
- You are offered a prestigious foreign training programme during a period when your district faces a public-health emergency.
- A private hospital refuses to treat an accident victim without advance payment; you have the authority to act but the law is ambiguous.
- In your study circle, a member is sharing leaked mock-test papers. Others are benefiting and ask you to stay quiet.
- A popular welfare scheme is fiscally unsustainable in your state. Recommending cuts is right on the numbers but will hurt vulnerable people now.
