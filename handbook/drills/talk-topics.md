# Talk topic bank

[← Handbook](../README.md)

For recorded 2-minute talks ([Communication & presence](../modules/L4-communication.md)): 30 seconds to plan
(Point – Reason – Example – Point), 2 minutes to speak. The app reads every bullet below.

## About you

- Why do you want to join the civil services?
- What is the biggest challenge facing your home district, and what would you do about it?
- Why did you choose Political Science and International Relations as your optional?
- Describe a time you led a group through a disagreement.
- What is one weakness you are actively working on, and how?
- What have you learned from setbacks in your preparation?
- Which public servant do you admire, and why?
- What do you do when you are not studying, and what has it taught you?
- If not the civil services, what would you be doing, and why?

## Issues

- Is India's demographic dividend at risk?
- What should be the priorities of India's foreign policy in the next decade?
- Should voting be made compulsory?
- How can trust between citizens and the police be improved?
- Is technology making governance more accountable or more distant?
- What would you change first in India's school education system?
- How should India handle the tension between development and environment?

## Abstract

- Not everything that counts can be counted.
- Freedom is not the absence of commitments.
- Character is what you do when no one is watching.
- The best way to predict the future is to create it.
- Silence is also an answer.
- Patience is bitter, but its fruit is sweet.
