# Thinking prompt bank

[← Handbook](../README.md)

The app reads this file directly: every bullet under a heading becomes a drill prompt. Add your own prompts
(from your study programme's current topics) as new bullets — keep one prompt per line.

Use any prompt with any thinking move from [Critical & multi-angle thinking](../modules/L3-thinking.md):
7 lenses, steelman, causal chain, stakeholder map, first principles, pre-mortem.

## Issues

- Should India move to simultaneous elections for Parliament and state assemblies?
- Is freebie politics a welfare tool or a fiscal risk?
- Should social media platforms be held liable for content posted by users?
- How should India balance coal-based energy security with climate commitments?
- Is the anti-defection law strengthening or weakening Indian democracy?
- Should the minimum age of marriage be the same for men and women?
- Can cooperative federalism survive fiscal centralisation?
- Should India adopt a universal basic income?
- Are coaching centres a symptom or a cause of problems in education?
- How should India regulate artificial intelligence in public services?
- Should urban local bodies have their own police and planning powers?
- Is the collegium system the right way to appoint judges?
- Should groundwater be treated as a public good with metered extraction?
- Does India need a national policy on internal migrants?
- Should India join or stay outside large regional trade agreements?
- Is the delimitation of constituencies on population a threat to federal balance?
- Should prisons focus on rehabilitation over punishment?
- How should India respond to rising heatwaves in cities?
- Can the gig economy offer decent work without stifling innovation?
- Should India expand nuclear power significantly?
- Are reservations in private-sector jobs desirable?
- Should India's foreign policy prioritise strategic autonomy over alliances?
- Is the Governor's office functioning as intended by the Constitution?
- How should public hospitals reduce out-of-pocket expenditure for the poor?
- Should mother-tongue instruction be mandatory in primary schools?

## District problems

- Floodwater has entered 40 villages overnight and two embankments are at risk.
- A viral video alleges caste discrimination in a government school's mid-day meal.
- Farmers are blocking the highway demanding compensation for crop loss.
- Dengue cases have tripled in a month in the district headquarters town.
- A land acquisition for an industrial park is being resisted by tribal gram sabhas.
- A rumour on messaging apps is triggering communal tension before a festival.
- The district's child sex ratio has fallen sharply in the latest data.
- A bridge built two years ago shows major cracks; the contractor is politically connected.
- Teacher absenteeism in remote schools is above 30%.
- Illegal sand mining at night is damaging the riverbed and endangering villages.
- Ration shops are reported to be diverting grain despite biometric authentication.
- Heatwave deaths among outdoor workers have been reported for the first time.

## Structure prompts

- Discuss the role of civil society in strengthening accountability in governance.
- Critically examine the effectiveness of Panchayati Raj institutions three decades after the 73rd Amendment.
- Analyse the challenges of urbanisation in India and suggest a way forward.
- Evaluate the role of technology in improving the delivery of welfare schemes.
- Comment on the relationship between economic growth and inequality in India.
- Discuss the ethical issues involved in the use of surveillance for public safety.
- Examine the significance of the Indian Ocean region for India's security.
- Critically analyse the role of the Election Commission in ensuring free and fair elections.
- Discuss the causes of farm distress and measures to address it.
- Examine the challenges in implementing the right to education.

## Calibration statements

- F | The Rajya Sabha can be dissolved by the President.
- T | The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act relates to Panchayati Raj institutions.
- F | The Comptroller and Auditor General of India is appointed by the Prime Minister.

- F | The Supreme Court of India was established in 1947.
- T | The Western Ghats are geologically older than the Himalayas.
- F | The Tropic of Cancer passes through Kerala.
- T | The Tropic of Cancer passes through Mizoram.

- T | John Rawls wrote "A Theory of Justice".
