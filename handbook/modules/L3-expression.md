# L3 · Clear, structured expression

[← Handbook](../README.md)

**Dimensions served:** 10 Clear, structured expression, 11 Communication, 8 Thinking.
**Evidence grade:** A (deliberate practice with feedback; automaticity reduces load) · B (structures as retrieval scaffolds).

## Why it matters for UPSC
An examiner reads hundreds of copies; a board hears dozens of candidates a day. "Clear and logical exposition"
is listed explicitly by UPSC. Structure is how good thinking becomes visible at speed.

## How it works
- **Deliberate practice** — targeted practice on weaknesses with immediate feedback — improves performance, though
  it explains only part of the differences between people (Ericsson et al., 1993; Macnamara et al., 2014).
- **Automatic structures free working memory** for content (Sweller, 1988).
- **Specificity**: practise at exam speed and in exam format.

## Structures to automate

| Structure | Use |
|---|---|
| **Intro – Body – Way forward – Conclusion** | Most Mains answers |
| **PREP** — Point, Reason, Example, Point | Short spoken answers; paragraphs |
| **What – Why – How** | Explaining a concept or scheme |
| **Context – Issue – Options – Recommendation** | Policy and administrative questions |
| **Directive words** — discuss, critically examine, analyse, evaluate, comment | Match the structure to the directive |

## Exercises & dose
- **1-minute structure drill (daily rotation):** read a prompt, spend 60 s writing only the skeleton (headings and
  one line each). Speed builds the habit.
- **Spoken PREP (3×/week):** 60-second answers to interview-style questions, recorded.
- **Rewrite drill (weekly):** take one of your own answers or drill responses and rewrite it shorter and clearer.
  Compare word counts and rubric scores.
- **Writing speed check (monthly):** words per minute at legible quality, hand-written, 10 minutes.

## Rubric (1–5 each)
Structure · Relevance to the question · Clarity (one idea per paragraph) · Specificity (examples, data, cases) ·
Conclusion quality.

## Progression
- **L1** Uses a structure when reminded.
- **L2** Skeleton in 60 s for most prompts.
- **L3** Structure automatic in writing and speech; directive words decoded instantly.
- **L4** Layered, crisp answers at exam speed.

## Objective markers
Structure rubric score · skeleton time · words/min at quality · AI rubric score.

## Common traps
- Beautiful structure with generic content.
- Ignoring the directive word.
- Practising only by reading model answers.
