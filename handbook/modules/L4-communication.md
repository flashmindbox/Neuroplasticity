# L4 · Communication & presence

[← Handbook](../README.md)

**Dimensions served:** 11 Communication & presence, 10 Expression, 6 Confidence, 3 Stress.
**Evidence grade:** A (graded exposure / stress inoculation; deliberate practice with feedback) · B (self-observation via recordings; reappraisal).

## Why it matters for UPSC
The Personality Test is 275 marks — a conversation with a board assessing mental alertness, clear exposition,
balance of judgement and composure. Top-10 candidates typically score well here; the difference between an
average and a strong interview is decisive at the top of the list.

## How it works
- **Graded exposure** reduces speaking anxiety and builds skill (stress inoculation — Saunders et al., 1996).
- **Recordings give honest feedback.** You cannot see yourself while speaking; playback reveals fillers, pace and habits.
- **Reappraisal** turns pre-speaking nerves into usable energy (Brooks, 2014).
- **Mastery ladders** build speaking self-efficacy step by step (Bandura, 1977).

## Exercises & dose

### 1. Recorded 2-minute talk (3×/week)
Pick a [talk topic](../drills/talk-topics.md). 30 s to plan (PREP), 2 min to speak, then play back. The app
counts words per minute and filler words from the transcript; you rate the rubric; optionally the AI rates the
content and structure (it cannot hear tone).

### 2. Speaking mastery ladder
Alone → recorded → to a friend → with follow-up questions → to a small group → AI mock board → human mock board.

### 3. Listening drill (weekly)
In one real conversation, summarise the other person's point before replying. Boards notice listening.

### 4. Graceful "I don't know" (weekly)
Practise: "I'm not certain about that, sir/ma'am. My understanding is… but I would want to check." Honesty is a
strength in front of a board.

### 5. AI mock board (Phase 4 weekly; occasionally earlier)
Board-style questions drawn from your profile (education, home state, hobbies, PSIR, service preferences),
followed by a scored debrief against UPSC's personality criteria.

## Rubric (1–5 each)
Clarity of main point · Structure · Balance · Pace & fillers (from metrics) · Presence (self/external).

## Progression
- **L1** 1-minute recorded talks; fillers > 8/min.
- **L2** 2-minute talks with a clear point; fillers falling.
- **L3** Calm pace (~120–160 wpm), concise answers, listening before replying.
- **L4** Composed, warm, conversational under questioning; strong mock-board scores.

## Objective markers
Fillers/min · words/min · rubric scores (self vs external) · mock-board scores.

## Common traps
- Memorised answers that sound rehearsed.
- Never listening to your recordings.
- Avoiding speaking until "ready".
